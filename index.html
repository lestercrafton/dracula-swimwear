What women tell us

Every wish is a pattern change

These are the things people say about suits they almost loved. In the fitting room, each one becomes a measured adjustment on your blueprint.

I wish the strap sat a little lower.→ strap anchor −1.5 cm on shoulder
I need more space in the front.→ cup pattern +2 cm, band unchanged
This wire is so hard.→ underwire swapped for flex wire
I wish the bottom were a bit longer.→ leg line lowered, coverage up
Straps always dig into my shoulders.→ strap width 1.2 → 2 cm
High-waist, but not up to my ribs.→ rise set 3 cm below natural waist
Cost-plus, shown line by line

You pay what it costs to make, plus one clear margin

Because every suit is priced from its own materials and labor, the studio can take ten orders or ten thousand without guessing. The margin keeps the lights on and is the same for everyone.

The Vamps

A Vamp bites into the fabric with her needle

Vamps are the sewists who make every Dracula suit: retired seamstresses, parents at home while the kids are at school, people whose jobs changed and who still have great hands. Each one is vetted, paid per suit at a fair hourly rate, and matched to orders near where she lives.

Your body, your photos, your phone
  • Photos are measured on your phone and deleted right after. They are never uploaded.
  • Scan in whatever you're comfortable in, including nothing. The app shows only an outline while it scans.
  • Your face appears on your avatar only if you add it, and only on your own device.
  • Vamps see measurements and a pattern, never your name, face or photos.
  • Delete your fit profile any time, in one tap.

    Becoming a Vamp

    1. Apply in the app: where you live, your machines, what you've sewn.
    2. Sew a paid test suit from a sample blueprint.
    3. Pass QA and a short video call with Alex.
    4. Get your starter kit: pattern paper, needles, labels, packaging.
    5. Pick up jobs near you. Take as many or as few as you like.

    Any fabric, any print

    Clients can ask for any color or pattern. The app turns the design into a seamless print tile, and a digital fabric printer prints it on swim-grade stretch fabric in small runs.

    1. Design approved → 300 DPI repeat tile generated

    4. Solids and linings kept in stock at the studio
